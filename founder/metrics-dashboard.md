# Five metrics for the pilot

Assumption: last recorded stage is pre-launch. All current values are unknown. Targets below are proposed decision gates, not external benchmarks.

## Weekly metrics

| Metric and definition | Day 30 / 60 / 90 target | If below |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified pilot purchase: actual payers / qualified people offered declared mission | 3/10; 5/15; 10/25 | Separate problem, trust, setup and price objections; change one offer variable |
| Safe correct task outcome: clear feasible benchmark journeys completed correctly / attempted; separately log unsafe/false done | >=95% each; 0 unsafe and 0 unsupported completions in sampled suite | Stop broader autonomous adoption; fix target/context/completion failures |
| Material finding acceptance: independently reproducible, acted-on or confirmed material issues / reviewed alleged material issues | >=80%, >=85%, >=90%; label sample sizes | Review unsupported claims and severity; improve assertions/evidence |
| Paid repeat within 30 days: purchasers buying/redeeming another paid mission / purchasers eligible for full 30-day window | >=20%, >=30%, >=40% | Determine whether need is one-off; keep PAYG and avoid forced subscription |
| Contribution margin: settled revenue minus payment/refund, model/data/compute/storage and variable support/review costs, divided by settled revenue | >=40%, >=50%, >=60% | Diagnose retries, labor, context or retention cost; revise quote/catalog before volume |

Legitimate broken-site outcomes are evaluated separately from clear feasible task completion. Do not blame the controller for a genuinely blocked fixture; do not treat paid-wallet redemption as fresh cash revenue. Pass purchases are not MRR.

## Ignore these as primary success measures

Total scanner findings, report word count, model tokens used, aggregate readiness score and signups without a completed mission. They can increase while trust and value fall. Investigate disputed issues and repairs instead.

## Minimal tracking

Use existing Supabase records, durable ledger/provider attempts and a founder CSV/dashboard, no new analytics purchase required. Estimate 1-2 days for basic internal queries after the new contracts exist. Keep only IDs/status/timing in events, not raw typed values/private screenshot contents.

Track 12 events: mission_scoped, quote_accepted, mission_started, auth_handoff, assertion_completed, mission_blocked, mission_finished, dossier_opened, issue_confirmed, issue_disputed, recheck_completed, usage_settled. Include workspace, mission, quote version, outcome and timing where appropriate. Add verified payment/refund records directly from existing billing; do not infer purchase from a clicked button.
