# Idea validation: release verification

Assumption: the last recorded stage is pre-launch and solo-founder. Current customer count and revenue are unknown. Prior September 24 targets are not actual traction.

## Assessment and verdict

**Pivot the promise, preserve the foundations.** Current code deliberately compresses report evidence and lacks business-result assertions. A capable builder has little reason to pay repeatedly for familiar audit flags plus an unreliable traversal. A scoped authenticated workflow, reproducible defect, useful fix and verified rerun is a plausible paid outcome. Willingness to pay remains unvalidated.

This supersedes the earlier broad bundle endorsement as the immediate product direction. See [master plan](../plan.md).

## Five fatal questions

1. **Who pays today?** Hypothesis: a SaaS founder releasing onboarding this week or an agency handing over a logged-in app. Not every AI builder. Test a proposed $19 founder pilot or $49 agency packet. These are experiment prices, not measured willingness to pay.
2. **Why hasn't this been built?** It has. [Checkly](https://www.checklyhq.com/pricing/) offers authored browser monitoring; [BrowserStack Test Companion](https://www.browserstack.com/test-companion) offers natural-language generation/diagnosis; [QA Wolf](https://www.qawolf.com/service/software-testing-company) combines agents and QA engineers. Published features do not establish their task success rates. Walkthru must prove setup speed, evidence and fix verification; a stronger model is not exclusive.
3. **Distribution advantage?** None demonstrated. Start with founder-run pilots in the existing network and agency handovers. A shareable, permissioned evidence packet may help referral, but neither referrals nor acquisition cost are measured. Do not assume SEO alone creates demand.
4. **Business or feature?** Generic auditing is easy to substitute. Saved suites, release history and verified fixes could support recurring use, but buyers may need only a one-off launch check. At an assumed $44 recurring ARPU, $1M ARR requires about 1,894 active subscribers. This arithmetic is not a market forecast.
5. **Can the founder build it?** The stack, extension, job queue and evidence exist. The hardest work is reliable state/target grounding, trustworthy assertions, financial admission and customer-specific setup. Concierge pilots are possible before a full autonomous platform. Do not disguise manual investigation as automated reliability.

## Three experiments

All success criteria are proposed, not industry benchmarks. No outreach, purchase or new checkout was performed.

| Experiment | Exact test | Pass criterion | Estimate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Founder launch check | 10 qualified founders releasing within 14 days; one authorized journey, reviewed evidence, one rerun for proposed $19 | 3 pay, 2 act on or confirm a material finding | 7 days, $0-100 excluding labor |
| Agency handover | 5 agencies delivering an app; one authorized staging packet for proposed $49 | 2 pay, 1 repeat purchase | 14 days, under $150 excluding labor |
