# Product brief: Walkthru

Full version with screenshots: `Walkthru-Briefing.pdf` at the repo root.

## 1. Problem
People who build apps with Cursor, Lovable, Bolt or v0 ship in days but cannot see a stranger use the product. They don't know if signup works for a first-time visitor, whether Google and AI search can read a JavaScript-built site, or whether a key leaked into public code. Today they ask friends, run Lighthouse and securityheaders.com separately, or pay for user-testing tools priced for companies.

## 2. Target audience
Solo founders and two-person teams, 0 to 3 months from launch, shipping an AI-built web app with a signup flow and no designer or QA. Secondary: small agencies handing sites to clients. Run `/persona-gen` for full personas.

## 3. Value proposition
Walkthru helps founders of AI-built apps launch without silent failures by running AI test users through real flows in their own browser and checking SEO, GEO and security in the same report, unlike AI user-testing tools, which test journeys only and need your credentials for logged-in pages.

## 4. Core features (MVP)
1. **Instant Scan:** URL in, report in about 20 s, no install. The free hook and the demo.
2. **AI test users in the owner's Chrome:** real journeys, logged-in pages, no password shared. The core differentiator.
3. **GEO readiness with "what AI search sees":** the sharpest reason to try it in 2026; deterministic, costs nothing.
4. **Grounded report with ranked fixes:** every finding cites evidence; trust is the product.
5. **Agent fix prompt:** turns findings into one paste for the coding tool; the main paid value.
6. **Rerun and compare:** proves the fix worked; the reason to come back.
7. **Server-owned plans and Dodo passes:** without them there is no business.

## 5. Success metrics (first 90 days, Estimates)
- 500 Instant Scans a week by day 90 (leading).
- 25% scan to signup; 40% install to finished run (leading).
- 30 paying customers by day 90.
- 60% of paid users rerun within 14 days (retention signal).

## 6. Risks and assumptions
| Assumption | Test (under $500, under 2 weeks) |
|---|---|
| Founders pay once around launch | A1: 30 public scans, $9 founding Launch Pack line; pass at 5 paid of 100 scans |
| The extension step does not kill the funnel | A2: 20 beta users; pass if 5 finish a run within 48 h |
| GEO is the stronger hook | A3: two headlines, $0 to $100 Reddit ads; pass at 1.5x scan starts |

## 7. Go-to-market snapshot
- Free scans posted in Indie Hackers, r/SaaS weekly thread, r/SideProject and builder communities. Estimate: $0 cash, about 1 hour per customer.
- Show HN and Product Hunt on 10-20. Estimate: $0.
- Agency outreach. Estimate: $0 to $50 per customer.
- Product-specific tactic: the Launch Ready badge on customers' sites, a permanent backlink.
